# Vedanta

A working library of Swami Medhananda (Ayon Maharaj) and related Vedanta scholarship.

| Book | Author(s) | Year | Format |
|------|-----------|------|--------|
| Infinite Paths to Infinite Reality: Sri Ramakrishna and Cross-Cultural Philosophy of Religion | Ayon Maharaj (Swami Medhananda) | 2018 | EPUB |
| The Bloomsbury Research Handbook of Vedanta | Ayon Maharaj (ed.) | 2020 | EPUB |
| Panentheism in Indian and Western Thought: Cosmopolitan Interventions | Benedikt Paul Göcke, Swami Medhananda (eds.) | 2023 | PDF |
| Swami Vivekananda's Vedantic Cosmopolitanism | Swami Medhananda | 2022 | PDF |

## Scope

Primary focus: Swami Medhananda's constructive Vedanta — Sri Ramakrishna's
vijñāna, Vedantic cosmopolitanism, and panentheism — with the Bloomsbury
handbook as the survey of the wider field.
